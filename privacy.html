Privacy & Data Protection

Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 2026 • We respect your privacy and never sell customer data.

01. Our Commitment to Your Privacy

V.R.A. Auto Detailing ("we", "us", or "our") is dedicated to protecting the privacy and personal information of our clients in Acworth, Kennesaw, Woodstock, and the greater Atlanta metro area. This Privacy Policy details the types of information we collect when you visit vra-autodetailing.com, communicate with us, or book detailing services.

02. Information We Collect

We collect only the minimal information necessary to deliver accurate quotes and perform mobile detailing on your vehicle:

Contact Details

Your name, phone number, and email address provided when you initiate a booking via SMS, Gmail, phone call, or WhatsApp.

Vehicle Data

Year, make, model, paint condition, interior upholstery type, and specific detailing preferences or concerns.

Service Location

Your home or workplace address within our 15-mile service radius (or beyond) where our mobile unit will perform detailing.

Device & Technical Info

Standard anonymized technical logs, IP address, device type, browser information, and local storage tokens for performance.

03. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide price quotes, confirm appointments, and dispatch our mobile detailing vehicle to your location.
  • To send arrival notifications, weather rescheduling updates, or service completion notices via SMS, WhatsApp, or phone.
  • To process invoices and issue payment receipts via Zelle or credit card processors upon completion.
  • To improve website performance, monitor server health, and deliver fast, dynamic content.

We Do NOT Sell or Trade Your Data:

V.R.A. Auto Detailing will never sell, rent, or lease your personal contact details, vehicle information, or service history to third-party advertisers, data brokers, or marketing lists.

04. Third-Party Tools & Service Providers

Our website interacts with trusted third-party technology providers to deliver functionality:

  • Sanity.io: Headless content management system used to host and deliver live package descriptions, prices, and before-and-after gallery photos.
  • OpenStreetMap & Leaflet: Interactive service radius mapping showing our 15-mile operating area around Acworth, GA.
  • WhatsApp / Meta & Google Gmail: Channels enabling direct client communication, fast messaging, and quote inquiries.
  • Cloudflare: High-performance hosting, DNS routing, SSL encryption, and DDoS protection.

05. Cookies & Local Storage

We use cookies and browser localStorage for practical user experience benefits:

  • Essential & Performance: Storing cached package prices locally to prevent page flickering and provide 0ms instant loading.
  • Cookie Preferences: Remembering whether you have acknowledged our cookie notice so we don't display it repetitively.

You can manage or disable cookies via your browser settings or via the Cookie Preferences link in our footer at any time.

06. Your Data Rights & Choices

Under applicable United States and Georgia privacy standards, you have the right to:

  • Request confirmation of what personal contact information we have on file for your vehicle or address.
  • Request the deletion or correction of your phone number, email, or booking history from our records.
  • Opt out of any non-transactional text messages by simply replying "STOP" at any time.
