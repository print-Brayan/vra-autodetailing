Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to provide water or power?
No, we are fully equipped with our own water tank and generator. We can detail your car at your home, apartment complex, or workplace seamlessly.
How long does a detailing service take?
Depending on the package and the condition of the vehicle, it can take anywhere from 2 hours for a Standard Detail to over 5 hours for a Full Detail.
What payment methods do you accept?
We accept Cash, Zelle, and all major Credit/Debit cards upon completion of the service.
