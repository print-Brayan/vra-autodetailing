02. Mobile Service Requirements & Access

V.R.A. Auto Detailing operates as a 100% self-contained mobile detailing unit equipped with our own pressurized water tank and electrical generator. To perform services safely and effectively:

  • The client must provide a safe, lawful, and accessible parking space (driveway, private parking bay, or authorized work lot) with at least 3 to 4 feet of clearance around the entire perimeter of the vehicle.
  • We reserve the right to decline or reschedule service if the location is deemed unsafe (e.g., steep hazardous inclines, busy narrow roadways, or areas where property managers prohibit detailing).
  • Vehicle keys and access must be provided at the scheduled appointment time.

03. Service Area & Travel Policy

Our standard service coverage includes a 15-mile radius from Acworth, GA (encompassing Acworth, Kennesaw, Woodstock, and adjacent areas) without travel surcharges.

Appointments scheduled outside this 15-mile radius are welcomed but will incur a standard per-mile travel fee disclosed and confirmed prior to appointment dispatch.

Clients are required to remove all personal items, cash, credit cards, electronics, firearms, hazardous materials, child car seats (or detach them), and high-value belongings from the vehicle prior to our arrival.

Auto Detailing is not responsible for loss, misplacement, or accidental damage to any personal property left inside the cabin, glove box, or trunk. Compartments containing private items may be bypassed during cleaning at the technician's discretion.

05. Pre-Existing Conditions & Damage Inspection

Prior to initiating service, a visual assessment will be performed. V.R.A. Auto Detailing shall not be held liable for:

  • Pre-existing paint defects, including swirl marks, deep scratches, rock chips, clear coat oxidation, etching from bird droppings, or improperly cured repaints.
  • Aged, brittle, loose, or cracked dashboard materials, clips, vents, or interior leather seams that tear or detach under standard manufacturer-recommended cleaning procedures.
  • Pre-existing water leaks, faulty door/window seals, or electronic sensor malfunctions caused by prior moisture intrusion.

06. Heavy Soiling, Pet Hair & Biohazards

All baseline package prices assume an average level of vehicle wear and dirt. Severe accumulation of pet hair, mold, mildew, food spills, vomit, bodily fluids, severe smoking odor, or chemical stains require specialized equipment, sanitation chemistry, and additional labor.

Any necessary price adjustments or add-ons (e.g., Pet Hair Removal, Odor Treatment, Deep Extraction) will be clearly communicated for client approval before proceeding.

07. Weather & Rescheduling Policy

Because we provide mobile on-site detailing, severe weather (lightning storms, heavy rain, sustained freezing temperatures, or high wind conditions) can affect the safety of technicians and the quality of finish (such as ceramic spray application or claying).

In the event of adverse weather, V.R.A. Auto Detailing reserves the right to reschedule your appointment to the earliest mutually available date at no additional fee or penalty.

08. Cancellations & Appointment Changes

We value your time and allocate dedicated time slots for each vehicle. If you must cancel or reschedule, please provide at least 24 hours advance notice via text or phone so we can accommodate other clients on our waitlist.
